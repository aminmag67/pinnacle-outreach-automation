EMAIL CHARACTERISTICS
================================================================================

WHAT WORKS (all examples above):
✅ Opens with a specific pain point
✅ No mention of "free reports" or generic offerings
✅ No dates, seasons, or time references
✅ Personalized to the industry
✅ Keeps benefits clear and relevant
✅ Single main CTA (visit website)
✅ Secondary CTA (schedule a call)
✅ Professional but conversational tone
✅ Signature included (name, title, website)
✅ No fancy punctuation or Unicode characters
✅ Plain ASCII text (no mojibake)

WHAT DOESN'T WORK (examples of emails the agent AVOIDS):
❌ "Get Your Free Branding Report"
❌ "Limited time offer - expires Friday"
❌ "January rush - automate NOW!"
❌ "Hi [FirstName]" (we don't have first names)
❌ "I've prepared a custom proposal for [Company]"
❌ All emoji and fancy symbols
❌ Long paragraphs (3+ sentences)
❌ Overpromising ("100% more leads guaranteed")
❌ Vague CTAs ("reach out sometime")

================================================================================
EMAIL BODY FORMULA (Applied in all examples)
================================================================================

1. HOOK (2-3 sentences)
   - State a pain point specific to their industry
   - Make it relatable ("you're busy with...")

2. TRANSITION (1-2 sentences)
   - Introduce the solution
   - Explain how it solves the pain point

3. VALUE PROP (1-2 sentences)
   - What Pinnacle does
   - Why it matters to them

4. CTA PRIMARY (1 sentence)
   - Visit website: "pinnaclecontentstudio.com"
   - Framed as exploration: "Check out...", "Head over to..."

5. CTA SECONDARY (1 sentence)
   - Offer: "Schedule a call"
   - Tone: "Worth a conversation", "Let's discuss"

6. SIGNATURE (5 lines)
   - Name
   - Title
   - Email
   - Website
   - Separator line

Total: 120-180 words, 1 minute read time
